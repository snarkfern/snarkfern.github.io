How ordering works

No new account, no risk – it all runs through Amazon.

1

Pick a design

Filter by hobby or season, or just search for your thing. Every design here is our own original work.

2

Choose size & color on Amazon

The button takes you straight to the shirt on Amazon, where you pick the fit, size and color.

3

Amazon prints & delivers

Each shirt is printed on demand and shipped by Amazon. Payment, delivery and returns run through your Amazon account.

A tiny studio with a big soft spot for hobbies.
